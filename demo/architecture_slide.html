ClauseGuard — conflict-aware GR drafting for Maharashtra (MAHA-GR-ALIGN)

Real corpus

776 GRs · 10,370 indexed clauses

Marathi GRs from the Internet Archive (live portal was down). Legacy-font garbling scored, filtered & digit-audit repaired.

Clause retrieval

e5-small → Chroma

Multilingual embeddings, boilerplate filter, near-duplicate suppression. Thousands of clauses → ~30 candidates.

Fine-tuned screening

MuRIL-base · 238M

Trained on 4,490 grounded clause pairs. 130 ms/pair on CPU — 30 candidates → ~3 flags, no API, no rate limit.

Verify & explain

LLM extracts · code decides

The LLM only extracts values ("50 टक्के" vs "100 टक्के"); deterministic comparison makes the call. Auditable reasons, not opinions.
